## Rules for what's in frame

1. **No real names, faces or handles other than your own.** Blur or replace
   them. A schedule is a location-by-hour map of a real person — that's the
   premise the whole privacy model in `SPEC.md` §6 is built on, and it does not
   stop applying because the screenshot is going on Instagram.
2. **Check the room numbers.** `shared-gaps.png` and `tut-06-free-now.png` are
   the two screens most likely to have a real building and time on them.
3. Status bar: full battery, no notifications, a plausible time — mid-morning
   reads better than 3am.
4. Light mode only. The dark palette exists but the whole campaign is cream.
5. If you have to fake data to keep someone's schedule private, fake it in the
   app with a second account rather than in Photoshop. It'll be right.
