# Screenshots

Renders of the real components, taken from a throwaway preview route against no
database at all.

**Every person, course and room in these images is invented.** That is the
point: a screenshot committed to a repo is a screenshot published, and a real
student's timetable, friends list and room numbers are the three things Honk
promises not to hand out. The names here belong to nobody.

- `2026-09-02-home.png` — the possessive heading, the friend count as a
  pressable control, "Your people" folded past three friends, the phone-only
  invite QR, and the nudge that points at a class in the week.
